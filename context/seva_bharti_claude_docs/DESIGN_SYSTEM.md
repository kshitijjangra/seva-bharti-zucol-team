# Seva Bharti Design System

## Reference
Use the supplied Seva Bharti logo as the primary visual reference.
Do not redraw, distort, recolour, or replace it without permission.

## Visual direction
- Respectful, clear, community-oriented presentation.
- Use the orange, blue, white, and warm neutral tones visible in the logo.
- Suggested starting colours: orange `#FF982F`, blue `#0789D1`,
  white `#FFFFFF`. Treat these as approximate visual starting points,
  not official brand specifications.
- Use dark, readable text on light backgrounds.
- Reserve strong orange and blue for accents, links, headings, and actions.
- Avoid excessive gradients, animations, shadows, and decorative effects.
- Do not introduce unrelated logos or stock photos.

## Typography
- Support Devanagari and Latin text.
- Choose legible fonts with proper Hindi glyph coverage.
- Verify rendering, line height, and wrapping on mobile devices.
- Do not transliterate or rewrite official Hindi content without approval.

## Layout
- Mobile-first responsive design.
- Consistent spacing and content width.
- Clear page hierarchy and accessible contrast.
- Use the supplied logo asset with its aspect ratio preserved.
- Use real photographs only when provided by the user.

## Accessibility
- Semantic landmarks and heading hierarchy.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation.
- Visible focus states.
- Descriptive image alt text when the image's content is known.
- Empty alt text for purely decorative images.
- Do not invent image descriptions.

## Content integrity
Visual polish must never introduce unsupported factual claims.
A placeholder is preferable to invented text.
